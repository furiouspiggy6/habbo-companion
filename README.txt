HABBO COMPANION v1.0.4 — AIR CONNECTION RECOVERY
===================================================
This release prevents unverified automatic startup of G-Earth from freezing Habbo.

- Fresh installs default to manual G-Earth connection.
- Previous auto-connect/auto-minimize settings are disabled ONCE on upgrade.
- Open G-Earth (safe) starts the saved beta without automatic connection flags.
- Launch Habbo asks you to confirm G-Earth says Waiting for connection.
- Connect viewer only AFTER Habbo has fully loaded into the room.
- Experimental auto-connect remains available as an explicit opt-in.
- G-Earth is never auto-minimized or attached during startup.
- Existing chat settings, history and saved packet rules are preserved.
- Manipulation is always OFF unless explicitly enabled.

This recovery update does not claim to fix the beta's automated handshake.
The known working manual connection remains the safe fallback.
